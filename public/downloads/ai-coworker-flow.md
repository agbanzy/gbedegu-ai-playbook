# The AI Coworker Flow
### How to work with AI like it's staff, not a search box
*Godwin Agbane · GBEDEGU '26 · August 2026*

---

## The mindset shift

Stop treating AI like Google (ask, get, leave). Start treating it like a capable junior colleague: **brief it, give it materials, review its work, and let it keep context.**

## 1. Set up its "desk" (one-time, ~1 hour)

1. Pick **one** assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini) and create a **Project / workspace** for your business.
2. Upload the documents a new hire would need: what you sell, prices, your best posts, your tone, FAQs, target customer description.
3. Write standing instructions: "Always write in my voice (warm, direct, no jargon). My customer is X. Never invent prices — ask me."
4. Save your 5 best role prompts (see the Prompt Handbook) inside the project.

Now every conversation starts with a coworker who already knows your business.

## 2. The daily flow (15–30 minutes)

**Morning brief (5 min).** "Here's what's on today: [3 items]. Draft the two emails, outline the proposal, and list what you need from me."

**Midday review (10 min).** Read its drafts like a manager: approve, edit, or send back with one line of feedback ("warmer", "shorter", "add the price table").

**Evening close (5 min).** "Summarize what we finished, what's pending, and pre-draft tomorrow's first task."

## 3. The briefing framework (for bigger tasks)

Brief AI the way you'd brief a contractor — six lines:

1. **Role** — who it should be for this task
2. **Goal** — what done looks like
3. **Materials** — paste or attach everything relevant
4. **Constraints** — deadline-level detail: format, length, tone, budget
5. **Exclusions** — what NOT to do ("don't contact anyone, don't invent numbers")
6. **Checkpoint** — "show me the plan / outline before executing"

## 4. Review gates (non-negotiable)

| Work type | Gate |
|---|---|
| Internal drafts, research, summaries | Skim and go |
| Anything public (posts, emails to clients) | You read every word before it sends |
| Money, legal, contracts, pricing | You decide; AI only prepares options |
| Personal data of customers | Don't paste it in at all |

## 5. Three starter automations (weekend projects)

1. **The lead machine:** enquiry (IG/WhatsApp/web form) → AI drafts a reply in your voice → lead logged to a sheet/CRM → day-3 follow-up draft → Monday-morning summary to you. *(Zapier/Make/n8n + your assistant — ₦0–$20/mo.)*
2. **The content engine:** one long piece per week (voice note, video, or article) → AI cuts it into posts, captions, clips and a newsletter → scheduled via Buffer.
3. **The inbox triager:** new emails → AI labels (lead / invoice / spam / urgent) → daily digest with suggested one-line replies.

## 6. Scaling: from coworker to team

- **Delegate the repetitive** to automations — they work 24/7 without salary.
- **Hire agents like junior staff** — research, first drafts, data entry, QA. Brief like interns, review like interns.
- **Systemize** — your winning prompts become SOPs (skills / custom GPTs) your whole team reuses.
- **The guardrail:** humans stay on money, legal, and anything public-facing. Reputation can't be regenerated.

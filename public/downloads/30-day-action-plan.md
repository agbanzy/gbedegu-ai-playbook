# Your 30-Day AI Action Plan
### From today's talk to a working AI-powered personal brand
*Godwin Agbane · GBEDEGU '26 · August 2026*

A few evenings per week is enough. Don't aim for perfect — aim for shipped.

## Week 1 — Foundations
- [ ] Pick **ONE** assistant (Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini / Grok) and create an account. If unsure and cost matters: Gemini's AI Plus is ₦7,450/month here (students: 12 months free).
- [ ] Create a Project/workspace; upload what a new hire would need (what you sell, prices, best posts, tone, FAQs).
- [ ] Write and save **5 role prompts** for your business (see the Prompt Handbook).
- [ ] Run your website/socials through **Pomelli** (labs.google/pomelli) or build a brand kit in **Canva** — lock ONE palette + font.

## Week 2 — Presence
- [ ] LinkedIn overhaul: AI-drafted headline, About, and Featured — then edit until it sounds like *you*.
- [ ] Make your name, photo, bio and handle consistent on every platform you use.
- [ ] Publish your first AI-assisted post (drafted with your role prompt, edited by you).

## Week 3 — Ship your site
- [ ] Prompt your assistant: *"Build me a one-page personal brand site: my name, what I do, three services, two testimonials, a contact section with a WhatsApp button. Navy & teal, modern, mobile-first, single HTML file."*
- [ ] Iterate in plain English until it feels right.
- [ ] Host it free: Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or GitHub Pages.
- [ ] Generate a QR code pointing to it; put it on your card, flyers, and event banners.
- [ ] Optional: buy your domain ($5–12/year, .me or .com.ng).

## Week 4 — Systems
- [ ] Build **one** automation: enquiry → AI-drafted reply → logged lead → day-3 follow-up → Monday summary (Zapier / Make / n8n).
- [ ] Start the content engine: one substantial piece weekly (voice note counts) → AI multiplies it into posts, clips and captions → schedule with Buffer.
- [ ] Review: what saved real time this month? Double down on that. Drop what didn't.

## The rules that make it stick
1. AI drafts — **you approve** anything public, legal, or money-related.
2. One paid tool at a time, and only when it passes a test: weekly limit hit, 2+ hours saved/month, or it touches revenue.
3. Consistency for 90 days beats brilliance for one week.
